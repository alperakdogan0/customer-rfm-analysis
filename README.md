# 📊 RFM Analysis for Customer Segmentation

Customer segmentation project using **Recency**, **Frequency**, and **Monetary (RFM)** analysis to identify high-value customer groups and support data-driven marketing strategies.

---

## 📖 Project Overview

Understanding customer behavior is essential for improving retention, increasing revenue, and optimizing marketing campaigns.

This project applies the RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) framework to transactional retail data, transforming raw customer purchase history into meaningful customer segments such as Champions, Loyal Customers, At Risk, and Potential Loyalists.

The entire workflow was also converted into a reusable Python function, allowing the analysis to be applied to different datasets with minimal modifications.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

How can a company identify its most valuable customers and create targeted marketing strategies based on purchasing behavior?

Instead of treating every customer equally, businesses can classify customers according to their purchasing patterns and allocate marketing budgets more effectively.

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Online Retail II Dataset**

## 🧮 RFM Metrics

### Recency
Days since the customer's most recent purchase.

### Frequency
Total number of unique purchases.

### Monetary
Total amount spent by the customer.
